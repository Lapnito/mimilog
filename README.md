## What is MimiLog?

**MimiLog** is a free, completely offline baby tracker for Android and iPhone, built for parents and caregivers of children from birth to age 3. It logs feedings, naps, diaper changes, pumping sessions, growth measurements (weight, height, head circumference) on the standard WHO percentile curves, and detailed body-temperature readings. Everything is saved in a local SQLite database on the phone — there is no account, no cloud, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no subscription. Ever.

Built for the 3 AM moments when the last thing you need is a paywall.

## Key Features

### Feeding
- **Breastfeeding timer** that remembers which side you started on last
- **Bottle log** in millilitres — breast milk, formula, mixed
- **Solids tracker** — note what your baby ate and how much
- **Automatic night-feed detection** between 10 PM and 6 AM

### Sleep
- **One-tap nap & overnight sleep** logging
- **Adaptive sleep schedule** that grows with your child from newborn to toddler
- **Total day sleep, night sleep and predicted next wake window** on a single screen

### Diaper
- Pee, poo, or both — one tap
- **Daily counter** with a gentle reminder if a newborn's wet-diaper count is low

### Pumping
- Left, right, or both
- Volume, duration and output rate per minute, session by session

### Growth & Health
- **Weight, height and head circumference** plotted on built-in **WHO percentile curves**, free, no paywall
- **Baby temperature log** with seven-level fever classification (hypothermia → dangerous) and a trend line
- Bath time, doctor visits, free-form journal notes

### Family
- **Multi-baby profiles** — twins, siblings, separate data
- **Shared timer** that survives a phone reboot
- **Statistics** by day, week and month

### Export
- Export to **CSV, Excel or JSON** straight to your file manager
- Carry a growth summary to a paediatrician visit, share a week of feeds with your partner

### Privacy
- **100 % on device** — every entry lives in a local SQLite database
- **No analytics, no advertising IDs, no third-party SDKs**
- **No signup, no email, no password**
- **Dark mode** for night feeds
- **One-handed UI** — every button big enough to tap with your free hand

## How MimiLog Works

**Q: Where is my data stored?**
A: In a local SQLite database on your phone. It never leaves the device.

**Q: Do I need an account?**
A: No. Open the app and start logging in under ten seconds.

**Q: Is it really free?**
A: Yes — free download, no ads, no in-app purchases, no premium tier, no subscription. Forever.

**Q: What if I want my data on my computer?**
A: Export to CSV, Excel or JSON straight to your file manager.

**Q: Can both parents use it?**
A: Yes, but each device keeps its own database (there is no cloud sync). You can export and share entries between devices manually.

**Q: From what age and to what age?**
A: From newborn through age 3 (36 months). Growth curves run for the whole 0–36 month range.

## FAQ

**Is MimiLog really free forever?**
Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no premium tier, no subscription.

**Does the app collect any data?**
No. There are no analytics libraries, no advertising identifiers and no third-party SDKs. Your baby's data lives in a local SQLite database on your phone.

**Does it work without internet?**
Yes. The app is offline-first — it works on a plane, in a hospital basement, anywhere without Wi-Fi.

**Can I track twins or siblings?**
Yes. Multi-baby profiles keep each child's data strictly separated.

**Are the growth charts the real WHO curves?**
Yes. Weight, height and head circumference are plotted on the standard WHO percentile curves from birth to 36 months.

**How does the night-feed detection work?**
Feeds logged between 10 PM and 6 AM are automatically marked as night feeds in statistics.

**Can I export the data?**
Yes — CSV, Excel or JSON, exported straight to your device's file manager.

**What's the minimum Android / iOS version?**
Android 6.0 (API 23) or later, iOS 13.0 or later.

**Is there a watch app?**
Not yet — MimiLog is currently phone-only.

**How do I report a bug?**
Open an issue at [github.com/Lapnito/mimilog/issues](https://github.com/Lapnito/mimilog/issues) or email tom@lapnito.cz.
