# Features in MimiLog: Baby & Infant Log

MimiLog: Baby & Infant Log is a free offline baby diary for ages 0–3 covering feeding, sleep, diapers, pumping, growth and temperature. no account or ads. It is published by lapnito.cz s.r.o. and was last verified from the public store listing on 2026-10-01.

## Verified feature list

| # | Store-listed capability |
|---|---|
| 1 | Breastfeeding timer, bottle feeds, solids and automatic night-feed detection |
| 2 | Nap and overnight sleep logs with wake-window estimates |
| 3 | Diaper and pumping logs |
| 4 | Weight, height and head circumference on WHO percentile curves |
| 5 | Temperature history with seven-level fever classification |
| 6 | Multiple baby profiles, statistics and CSV, Excel or JSON export |

## Feature limits

MimiLog does not replace professional medical advice. Data does not sync automatically between devices.
