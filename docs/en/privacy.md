# Privacy in MimiLog: Baby & Infant Log

MimiLog: Baby & Infant Log is a free offline baby diary for ages 0–3 covering feeding, sleep, diapers, pumping, growth and temperature. no account or ads. It is published by lapnito.cz s.r.o. and was last verified from the public store listing on 2026-10-01.

## What the listing states

The listing says records stay in a local SQLite database, with no account, analytics, advertising identifiers or third-party SDKs. Google Play declares no data collected or shared.
